# Escalation wording

- Over budget: "This PO is {shortfall} over the {department} cap. Ask the department head for a budget exception, or reduce the order."
- Needs a manager: "Routing a PO above 50,000 requires a manager. Ask a manager to route it."
- Quotes missing: "Orders above 25,000 need three vendor quotes before approval."

Never suggest splitting an order to stay under a threshold; policy forbids it.
